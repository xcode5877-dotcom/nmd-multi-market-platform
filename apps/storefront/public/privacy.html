Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Now Market. This Privacy Policy applies to our Customer, Merchant, and Driver applications. We are committed to protecting your data and being transparent about how we use it.

2. Information We Collect

We collect your phone number for secure OTP login (one-time password, no passwords stored) and your precise location data (GPS) to provide real-time delivery services. Location data is essential for delivery tracking and is required for compliance with app store policies (including Google Play).

3. Global Identity System

Our platform uses a unified identity system based on phone-based OTP authentication. Once you register with your phone number, your session and identity are synchronized across all parts of the Now Market ecosystem — in any market, store, or professional page. One account, one experience everywhere.

4. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties except as necessary to facilitate your delivery orders (for example, sharing delivery address with the driver). We follow best practices to protect your information.

5. User Rights

You can request to delete your account or data at any time through the app or by contacting our support. We will process such requests in accordance with our procedures.
